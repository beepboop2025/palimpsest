Palimpsest Evidence Desk editorial · source record as of 2026-08-30

BRI needs a balance sheet, not an announcement counter

Trade, construction, lending, utilization, fiscal cost and local welfare are different ledgers. The gap between them is where the real analysis starts.

Scale without a consolidated balance sheet

China's 2025 BRI-country trade aggregate is enormous, but an aggregate relationship with a class of countries is not a project return. Exports, imports, direct investment and contracted construction revenue belong in separate columns; none is a substitute for an audited schedule of lending, debt service, project cash flow and host-country fiscal cost.

The analytical discipline is stocks versus flows and commitments versus observed operation. A road can be physically complete while its traffic is thin; an investment stock can coexist with weak current cash generation; contracted revenue for a Chinese builder can be a liability or fiscal burden somewhere else.

[1] [2]

The upside is real enough to measure—and conditional enough to audit

The World Bank's corridor model supplies a serious pro-integration case: shorter travel times can support trade, investment and income. It also supplies the necessary warning label. Gains can disappear when infrastructure cost, debt stress, stranded capacity, corruption, environmental damage and displacement are omitted.

The correct scorecard therefore nets observed logistics and welfare gains against financing, operating, environmental and legitimacy costs. It does not choose between an official success narrative and a blanket failure narrative before the denominators arrive.

[3]

A rights-adjusted project return is an economic measure

Consultation, land rights, civic freedom and credible remedy affect construction delay, guarding, insurance, utilization and the durability of concessions. That does not turn every rights allegation into proven project causality. It means political legitimacy is a balance-sheet variable that should be investigated rather than edited out.

Palimpsest's proposed project scorecard keeps physical completion, utilization, external-account return, local distribution, consultation, displacement, civic freedom and evidence freshness in separate fields.
