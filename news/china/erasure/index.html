How to cite

Export the row. Quote the trail. Do not upgrade the claim.

  1. Find the row by title, gazetteer term, or source URL.
  2. Copy first-seen, last-seen, the snapshot or lookup URL, and the SHA-256.
  3. Download the CSV or JSON if you need the full table.
  4. Cite Palimpsest as the observatory that recorded a public disappearance, not as a witness inside China and not as proof of motive.
