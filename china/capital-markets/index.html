REST and MCP

Retrieve the reading and its broader evidence state

The static REST reading is the narrowest source:

curl -sS https://palimpsest.info/readings/stock-connect-latest.json \
  | python3 -m json.tool

For the current evidence desk—including coverage gates and abstention—use the release-bound MCP 1.9.3 server:

curl -sS https://api.seiche.info/palimpsest/mcp \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "jsonrpc":"2.0",
    "id":1,
    "method":"tools/call",
    "params":{
      "name":"get_signal",
      "arguments":{"name":"china-economic-pulse","max_rows":25}
    }
  }'

Do not infer a flow direction from northbound turnover. Do not treat southbound arithmetic net as investor nationality, domestic output or a causal signal.

FAQ

What this channel does—and does not—mean

Does Palimpsest cover all Chinese capital markets?

No. Its current dedicated market channel is official HKEX Stock Connect aggregate statistics. It does not claim comprehensive equities, bonds, derivatives, issuance, positioning or investor-level coverage.

Can northbound Stock Connect net flow be calculated from the current data?

No. HKEX discontinued the northbound buy and sell split in August 2024. Palimpsest publishes northbound turnover and never estimates a northbound net-flow direction.

Does Stock Connect turnover reveal investor nationality or the Chinese economy's direction?

No. Stock Connect is one cross-border market channel. Turnover does not identify beneficial-owner nationality, prove causation, measure domestic output, or support an economy-wide forecast.

Cite the current stock-connect-latest.json reading and its clocks. This page explains the boundary; it is not the measurement.
