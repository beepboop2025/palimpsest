What the chain still cannot prove

Hash-chain verification cannot establish that a refusal classifier measures the intended construct, that an endpoint label names fixed hidden weights, or that the panel represents all models. Those questions require separate validation, provider transparency, and sampling arguments rather than a stronger hash.Receipts evalevidence-d8f243177f1ebc287431, evalevidence-dd997047a6f3c3e7303b
