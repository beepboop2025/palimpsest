Information Erasure Observatory

What the record lost, sealed so it cannot be lost again

Most censorship trackers answer one question: is this reachable right now? Palimpsest answers a different one. It measures what was removed or rewritten, across three layers of the record, and hash-chains every reading at the moment of capture so our own account of the erasure cannot be quietly edited afterwards. We observe from outside the wall and put no one inside it at risk.

An access index measures

Whether a site, an app or a tool is reachable at this instant. That is a real and useful measurement, and it is a snapshot. It cannot tell you that a page got shorter, that an encyclopedia entry moved to the state line, or that a model stopped giving an answer it used to give.

This observatory measures

What existed and is now gone or altered. Anyone can publish a live number, so the number alone proves nothing. The hard part is proving you did not edit your own history after the fact, and that is what the ledger below is for.

The three layers

The composite is the mean of the layers that reported. A layer that is not reporting is shown as absent, with its reason, and is never folded in as a zero. A zero would say the record lost nothing on that layer, which is a different claim from having no measurement at all.

Independent cross checks

Separate instruments, measured by other people, pointed at the same thing. They do not feed the composite. They exist so that a layer moving on its own can be told apart from the world moving.

How this differs from an access index

The claim

Proving the measurement was not edited is the product

An observatory of erasure that can quietly revise its own past readings is not evidence of anything. It is a blog with charts. The ledger is what turns a number into something a sceptical reader can hold us to.

An access observatory measures whether a site, app or tool is reachable at this instant.
The Erasure Observatory measures what existed and is now gone or altered: a page that got shorter, an encyclopedia entry rewritten to the state line, an answer a model used to give and now refuses.
Anyone can publish a live number so the number alone proves nothing. The hard part is proving you did not edit your own history after the fact.
Our answer every reading is sealed into a public, append-only, tamper-evident ledger at capture time. Recompute the chain yourself. A single altered or dropped past reading fails to verify.

Method

Vantage-insensitive. Each layer ingests already-public data: OONI's aggregated measurements, the open encyclopedic record, and a panel of state-aligned models queried from outside the wall. No person inside China is ever asked to act. Judgement is lexical and auditable from the text. The composite is the mean of the layers that reported, and absent layers are shown as absent rather than zero-filled. Grounded in the trusted-public-archive literature (ARCHANGEL, arXiv:1804.08342) and the LLM-censorship measurement work (arXiv:2505.17441, arXiv:2506.12349). Not affiliated with OONI or Censored Planet. We ingest their open data with thanks.
