Interpretation boundary

What this surface refuses to claim

  • No metric is an estimate of true GDP.
  • Temporal order is not a causal or leading-indicator claim.
  • Not collected is not zero and source silence is not economic contraction.
  • Coverage and revision history do not yet support a broad state-of-economy composite.
  • No public source replicates China Beige Book's proprietary respondent panel, qualitative interviews, or shadow-finance microdata. This registry maps lawful aggregate and licensed substitutes; it must never be described as CBB data.
