Tor Metrics estimates 2,620 bridge users connecting from China

Bridge and transport estimates show demand for alternate routes without identifying individual users.

By Palimpsest Observatory21 Aug 2026 · 05:47 UTCAutomated evidence brief

2,620 estimated usersbridge users

What the record says

Finding. Tor Metrics estimates 2,620 bridge users for China in the current reading.

This report is scoped to circumvention-demand. It does not merge unlike instruments or infer a cause from co-movement.

How it was measured

Bridge users = demand for unlisted entry points (the demand-to-circumvent series). Relay users = direct reachability, structurally near-floor behind the GFW. A transport halving while another surges (transport_shifts) is the public fingerprint of a new GFW classifier deployment. Estimates are Tor's, from sampled directory requests; transport rows keep Tor's low/high bounds. No probe traffic is generated by this collector.

What this cannot establish

  • Bridge-user values are privacy-preserving estimates and should not be read as exact people or devices.
