Signal · In-path interference

Three findings, kept apart on purpose.

OONI's public aggregation for China over a ninety day window. Devices rewriting HTTP on the path. The transport that real-time voice and circumvention depend on. And measurement methods that never complete a single run inside the country. These are three different kinds of observation, and collapsing them into one index would destroy what each of them says.

Finding 1 of 3

Interference you can see

These tests do not ask whether a site is blocked. They send deliberately malformed or oddly cased HTTP and check whether anything on the path rewrote it before it arrived.

A non-zero rate is positive evidence of a device in the middle, whether or not that device chose to block anything.

Finding 2 of 3

Degradation you can measure

Whether the plumbing that real-time voice and pluggable-transport circumvention depend on is usable at all. STUN reachability for WebRTC, a stock Tor bootstrap, and Tor carried over Snowflake.

This is a health reading on transport. It is not a claim about any single website, and it is not the same measurement as the Great Firewall reachability index.

Finding 3 of 3

Measurement that cannot happen

Some test methods ran inside China this window and never once produced a result. That is not a low anomaly rate. It is no anomaly rate at all, because OONI's anomaly counter only ranges over runs that completed.

Drawing this as zero percent would invert it. Zero percent reads as clean. What it actually says is that the method cannot be carried out here, so nothing can be read from it in either direction.

So this finding is never given a bar and never given a percentage.

How the denominator was chosen

The rule, as the collector states it

loading

anomaly_count ÷ completed_count   is a rate.
anomaly_count ÷ measurement_count   is not.

Why it matters here

Reading the three marks

  • Solid fill: anomalies as a share of runs that completed. A real rate.
  • Hatched fill: runs that never produced a result, as a share of runs attempted.
  • Dashed void, no bar: no rate exists. Nothing completed, so there is nothing to take a share of.

Every mark on this page is labelled in words as well as in colour and shape, so none of the three depends on colour to be told apart.
