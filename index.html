04 / Publishing gate

How an eval becomes an article.

Full integrity model
  1. 00

    Why the founder started this work

    Palimpsest's founder began testing AI systems after seeing Chinese and state-aligned language models change, withhold or replace answers when prompts criticised the Chinese Communist Party or asked about documented events. A screenshot could capture one response, but it could not show whether the questions were fixed first, whether the pattern persisted, or whether the record was later edited. That observation became a repeatable eval and then a registry. It does not prove motive or describe every Chinese model. Read the full account, then inspect the current machine-readable assurance report.

  2. 01

    Freeze the test

    The probe set is hashed before any model response exists. A result without a prior registration does not enter the publication path.

  3. 02

    Recompute the record

    Published transcripts must reproduce the sealed response hash, labels, controls, and reported metrics.

  4. 03

    Write from receipts

    Article sentences are assembled from typed findings. Every analytical sentence points to an exact selector and value.

  5. 04

    Keep the attack surface

    Limitations, a counterreading, and a falsifier ship with the conclusion. Failed gates produce a visible warning or abstention.

  6. 05

    Revise without erasing

    A later run creates a content-addressed revision. The previous revision remains retrievable and linked.
