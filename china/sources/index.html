Registry boundary

No public source replicates China Beige Book's proprietary respondent panel, qualitative interviews, or shadow-finance microdata. This registry maps lawful aggregate and licensed substitutes; it must never be described as CBB data.

Palimpsest collects public aggregate data or contractually licensed aggregate outputs only. It never recruits respondents in China, stores respondent rows, bypasses access controls, or scrapes a service whose terms prohibit automation.
