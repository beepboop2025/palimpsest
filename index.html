Palimpsest / an open, tamper-evident public record

Follow the economy.Read the record.

Palimpsest is an open observatory of public records. It measures censorship and information control, keeps a rights-aware record of China’s economy, and pre-registers AI model evaluations so later edits are detectable. Every reading carries its source date, receipt and limit.
