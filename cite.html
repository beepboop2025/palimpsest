How to cite a specific signal on a specific day

Name the dataset id, the file, the generated_at clock, and the day you accessed it. A homepage citation cannot be checked. This page builds a BibTeX record from the Evidence Atlas. It does not invent a reading that is not in the catalog.

Cite one day

    
A day citation has to hit a history row. From a clone:

    
python3 -m scripts.build_citation_pack --dataset ddti --day 2026-08-22

    
If that day is not in the history file, the command abstains. It does not pick the nearest day.
