Finding 3 of 4

Two instruments, two denominators

OONI measures from probes run by volunteers inside China. Censored Planet measures remotely, using DNS and HTTP side channels from tens of thousands of vantage points outside it. Different technique, different population, different window.

Both detect Chinese interference. Their numbers are not comparable, and they are never averaged, added or presented here as one figure.

What the agreement buys is corroboration, not precision.

Why anomaly rate and not confirmed blocking

OONI marks a measurement confirmed when it sees an unambiguous block page. The Great Firewall rarely serves one. It injects a forged DNS answer or a TCP reset instead, so the connection simply fails and nothing announces itself as censorship.

Against China, confirmed sits near zero while real blocking is widespread. Tracking confirmed would report almost nothing. So this page tracks the anomaly rate, and states plainly that an anomaly is evidence of interference rather than proof of a policy decision.

We observe, we do not probe

Every figure on this page is computed from data somebody else already published. Palimpsest sends no request to a censored resource, runs no probe inside China, and asks no person there to do anything. These readings are vantage insensitive: running the collectors from anywhere produces the same numbers.
