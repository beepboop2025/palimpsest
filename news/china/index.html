Every retained current-window item whose reviewed source or feed metadata places it in the China/Hong Kong remit. This is broad monitored-feed coverage, not a claim to index the entire web.

Each entry reuses its validated event analysis. Multiple entries from one independence group do not increase corroboration. Only feed title, canonical URL, publication time, and a bounded excerpt are retained; article bodies are not copied or fetched.

Publisher reports remain attributed and are not converted into Palimpsest findings. Telegram aggregates remain unverified context. Combine reports with Observatory measurements · Feed directory · Standards · Source registry.
