# Institutional evidence routing

Reviewed 2026-09-23 UTC, after the financial-product portfolio work.

Palimpsest remains a public-good evidence and verification product. Its useful
contribution to an institution's research is source-bound China economic and
regional context, information-availability evidence and inspectable AI-evaluation
records. A financing announcement supplies no new authority for these records.

## Start with the user's question

| Question | Primary route | What stays attached |
|---|---|---|
| Is stress building at a named bank or other financial institution? | [LiquiLens institutional workflow](https://liquilens.in/developers/institutions/), then its exact entity/coverage tools | Institution identity, jurisdiction, filing period, sources, missingness and human review |
| What is happening in system funding or money markets? | [Seiche](https://seiche.info/money-markets/) and its source-health/context tools | Source clocks, units, market scope and as-published limitations |
| What does the public China or regional record show? | Palimpsest `research_catalog`, then the cited publication-checked dataset | Observation/release/capture times, revisions, rights and aggregate-versus-project scope |
| What does a published AI evaluation establish? | Palimpsest's sealed evaluation records and verification instructions | Frozen suite, controls, denominators, revisions, limitations and independent-validation status |
| What is the liquidity evidence for an asset and size? | [Undertow](https://liquilens-undertow.com/developers/) | Exact asset/size, venue coverage, depth clocks, currency conversion and executable-versus-research boundary |

## A source-bearing handoff

1. Discover available datasets using the current catalog; do not infer coverage
   from a product name or a country's presence in a registry.
2. Pass the relevant original publisher, source URL, unit, economic period,
   release/capture clocks and rights status into the reviewer document.
3. Keep Palimpsest findings separate from LiquiLens institution evidence and
   Seiche system-funding context. Explain a possible connection as a research
   hypothesis, not an automatically established cause or a shared risk score.
4. Preserve unavailable, restricted and stale states. The availability of NBS
   statistical tables does not grant rights to restricted CFETS/ChinaMoney data.
5. Keep a human review owner. An AI-evaluation receipt establishes the bounded
   evaluation record; it does not certify a bank's regulatory compliance.

OpenClaw, Hermes and other financial agents can use the LiquiLens installation
guide for the institution workflow and add Palimpsest for a relevant source
question. There is no reason to call the entire fleet for every user request.
The current Palimpsest MCP/API release contracts remain authoritative; this
guide adds no tool, score, collection target, publication entitlement or model.

Subscriber startup-funding material is internal product research. It is not a
Palimpsest censorship signal, public company dossier, evaluation label or
training corpus. The editorial and evidence publication rules remain unchanged.
