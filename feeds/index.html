Reading labels

Five kinds of item

The label tells you what kind of responsibility Palimpsest takes for the item.

Palimpsest result

A reproducible output from a named instrument or sealed eval.

Palimpsest availability

An instrument record that explicitly publishes no current result. Unavailable does not mean zero, normal or unchanged.

Palimpsest analysis

A bounded interpretation with citations, countercases and limits.

Source report

A publisher's claim, kept attributed and linked to the original.

Unverified context

A reviewed lead for follow-up that does not count as corroboration.
