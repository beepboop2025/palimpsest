When the official record changes

These cases cite historical official announcements. The live watch starts from its own captured baseline and keeps later document changes separate from those historical accounts.

2024-01-17

The youth unemployment series changed its population definition

NBS announced an age-group unemployment publication suspension in August 2023. In January 2024 it announced age-group rates excluding students and a new 25–29 age group.

Comparisons across the break need a consistent population definition. A lower rate after the change does not by itself measure an improvement among the same people. NBS attributed the change to refining its labor-force statistics.

Evidence capture: captured

2024-12-27

GDP revisions combine census evidence and a housing-services method change

NBS revised the preliminary 2023 GDP estimate using the fifth economic census and a simultaneous change to the valuation of owner-occupied urban housing services.

Separate the census revision from the accounting-method effect when comparing output vintages. This is a documented statistical revision; it is not itself evidence that output was fabricated.

Evidence capture: captured

Official record 1 · Official record 2
2024-11-29

Unemployment rates leave job quality and inactive workers outside their headline

NBS explains that survey unemployment concerns the labor force and that people neither searching nor available for work are outside that denominator. Its methodology distinguishes unemployment from hours and employment quality.

Read unemployment alongside hours, earnings, participation and working-age population. A stable headline alone cannot settle whether work is becoming more secure or better paid.

Make the investigation falsifiable

A gap, revision or disagreement can generate a research question. Establishing concealment requires evidence of what was withheld or changed, by whom, and why.

Test the demand story

Compare production, domestic orders, export orders and EU-reported demand by product and month.

Check alternatives: Inventory rebuilding, seasonal patterns and goods sold outside Europe can explain divergence.

Test the cash-flow story

Follow receivable days, inventory turnover and margins; compare the same calendar period across years.

Check alternatives: Industry composition and changes in the above-size enterprise sample can move aggregate ratios.

Test the property story

Track consecutive city price falls alongside official construction, sales and investment tables.

Check alternatives: A price index does not measure transaction volume, developer liquidity or household equity losses.

Test a publication-change claim

Inspect the before/after capture receipts, official methodological explanations and repeated checks from the watch.

Check alternatives: Routine revisions, site migrations and index rotation are alternative explanations; intent needs additional evidence.

Connect the regional record

Use Pakistan and Myanmar mirror trade with CPEC, Balochistan, Gwadar and BRI reporting to identify questions for project documents.

Check alternatives: National trade does not identify a particular port, district, financing contract or illicit actor.

Follow the regional consequences

Bring these questions into CPEC project finance, Gwadar utilisation, Balochistan livelihoods, BRI debt and Myanmar border economies. NarcoScope’s separately attributed evidence is available in the same research workflow.
