Forecast bands covered 84.3% of 1,559 scored readings

Prequential scoring shows whether the observatory's uncertainty bands remain calibrated over time.

By Palimpsest Observatory22 Aug 2026 · 07:23 UTCAutomated evidence brief

84.3%empirical coverage · across 1,559 forecasts

What the record says

Method. Empirical forecast coverage is 84.3% across 1,559 scored forecasts.

This report is scoped to forecast-ledger. It does not merge unlike instruments or infer a cause from co-movement.

How it was measured

strictly prequential (Dawid 1984): each reading is forecast from only its own past, never refit with hindsight. Point forecast = previous value (random walk). Band = adaptive conformal quantile of past absolute one-step changes, updated by alpha_{t+1} = alpha_t + gamma_t*(alpha - err_t) with decaying gamma (ACI, arXiv:2106.00170; decaying steps, arXiv:2402.01139), valid under arbitrary distribution shift. Scored by the Weighted Interval Score (arXiv:2005.12881), a proper rule, against the same forecast using a fixed quantile. Lower WIS is better.

What this cannot establish

  • Calibration describes aggregate historical coverage and does not guarantee that any one next reading falls inside its band.
