The operating contract

What “continuous” means here

Independent collectors run on cadences matched to their sources, from hourly to daily, weekly and monthly. This roll-up checks their committed readings hourly. The browser recomputes age against each source deadline, so a board can become stale without waiting for somebody to republish the page.

One failed collector cannot erase the last verified reading. It becomes stale or missing, remains visible in the coverage map, and lowers the board’s health.

Safety lines this surface will not cross

  • Public-source reads only; no targeting of people.
  • No offensive action, hack-back or third-party deception.
  • No synthetic fallback wearing a live timestamp.
  • No single fused rate when independent vantages materially disagree.
