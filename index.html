AI assistance, named

Automation is useful when its responsibility is visible.

AI assistance accelerated this rebuild and supports parts of the development workflow. It is not presented as evidence. Published surfaces distinguish original sources, deterministic analysis, machine-authored text and human review; the code and tests are public.

Palimpsest's founder began the evaluation work after a screenshot showed a state-aligned model changing or withholding an answer about criticism of the Chinese Communist Party. One screenshot could not show a repeatable pattern and does not prove motive, so the observation became a frozen evaluation and a revision-safe registry.
