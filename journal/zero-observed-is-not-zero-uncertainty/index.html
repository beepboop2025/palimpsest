Zero observed refusals is not zero uncertainty

4 control-clean model runs observed 0 refused families out of 34, yet each zero result still carries a Wilson 95% upper bound of 10.2%. That interval is part of the finding, not fine print.

Thesis

A finite eval can observe no refusals and still leave a meaningful range of plausible rates.

Evidence receipts
01

What zero contains

In the 16 Aug 2026 panel, 4 control-clean runs observed zero refused families among 34 monitored non-control families. For a zero-of-34 result, the published Wilson 95% interval still reaches 10.2%. Zero observed events and zero plausible event rate are different statements.Receipts evalevidence-89d8780f898337e3787c, evalevidence-e00da8ff59ae037434fc, evalevidence-e4d8af3496e0ac6acdd0

02

The unit is a question family, not a prompt

Each monitored question can appear in several meaning-preserving wordings, but the family is the statistical unit. That prevents a model from looking artificially precise merely because the same idea was phrased many times.Receipts evalevidence-e00da8ff59ae037434fc

03

Cross-lab agreement is useful and bounded

The latest panel contains 4 named endpoints, of which 4 passed every ordinary control. Agreement across those endpoints is stronger than a one-model anecdote, but it is not a probability sample of all models, deployments, languages, or future releases.Receipts evalevidence-e00da8ff59ae037434fc, evalevidence-e4d8af3496e0ac6acdd0

04

Read it as a dated panel, not a leaderboard

The registry binds every score to a model label, prompt commitment, response hash, and timestamp. Those receipts make change auditable, but they do not justify a permanent ranking from one sweep.Receipts evalevidence-89d8780f898337e3787c, evalevidence-e4d8af3496e0ac6acdd0

The strongest counterread

  • Observing zero refused families in 4 clean runs is real favorable evidence within this suite.

    evalevidence-e4d8af3496e0ac6acdd0

  • The nonzero upper interval is not evidence that hidden refusals occurred. It is the uncertainty left by a finite sample.

    evalevidence-e4d8af3496e0ac6acdd0, evalevidence-e00da8ff59ae037434fc

What this cannot establish

  • The monitored families were selected for an eval, not sampled from all questions people ask.

    evalevidence-e00da8ff59ae037434fc

  • A provider may change routing or model behaviour after the dated sweep.

    evalevidence-e4d8af3496e0ac6acdd0, evalevidence-89d8780f898337e3787c

  • A clean control state permits interpretation of the suite result but does not validate the lexical classifier against independent human coders.

    evalevidence-e00da8ff59ae037434fc
