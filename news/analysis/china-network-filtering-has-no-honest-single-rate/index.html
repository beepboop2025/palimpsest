OONI anomaly index reports 57.4 percent across 246,173 completed measurements, OONI in-path middlebox index reports 1.29 percent across 9,711 completed middlebox tests, and Inside-China fixed DNS panel reports 1.0 ratio across 5 qualifying answered measurement domains. Limitations

  • The instruments observe different test populations with different denominators and units.
    consequence
    No weighted average or single national percentage is calculated.
    limitation id
    limitation-incompatible-denominators
  • Probe, resolver, domain and remote-vantage coverage is not a probability sample of China's people or networks.
    consequence
    Findings are bounded to the named instruments and collection windows.
    limitation id
    limitation-vantage-coverage
  • OONI reachability and in-path metrics reuse the same publisher lineage.
    consequence
    They count as one independent group at the publication gate.
    limitation id
    limitation-shared-lineage
  • Rights-ineligible candidates excluded from numeric republication: censored-planet (LINK_ONLY/metadata_only).
    consequence
    An available source is not republished or counted when its current contract permits metadata-only reuse.
    limitation id
    limitation-rights-gated-candidate
