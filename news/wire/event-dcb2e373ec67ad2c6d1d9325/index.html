the Li Keqiang composite (40% loan growth, 40% electricity, 20% rail freight, using the canonical weights) against the state's own headline industrial-production YoY for the same month. Method and receipt

two prices for one currency: the state's 09:15 Beijing fix vs an independent same-day market reference (ECB concertation, 14:15 CET = 20:15 Beijing). gap_pct > 0 means the market prices the yuan weaker than the fix; the band is ±2%, so gap_pct is read against that scale.
