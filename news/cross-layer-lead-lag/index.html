What the record says

This report is scoped to cross-layer. It does not merge unlike instruments or infer a cause from co-movement.

How it was measured

each signal collapsed to a daily mean and DIFFERENCED (day-over-day change), then for every CROSS-layer pair the lag in [-7,7] days maximising |Pearson r|. Significance against a CIRCULAR-SHIFT null: one series is rotated against the other over every admissible offset (shifts within 7d of zero excluded), which preserves each series' own autocorrelation while destroying true alignment; p = (#{null >= observed}+1)/(n+1), exact and deterministic. Holm-Bonferroni step-down at alpha=0.05 across all pairs.

What this cannot establish

  • Lead-lag association can establish temporal co-movement but cannot establish that one layer caused another.
